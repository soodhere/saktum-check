# Design-partner pilot agreement (template)

> Template only. Have a lawyer review before use.

Between **[Company]** ("Partner") and **[Saktum entity]** ("Saktum").

1. **Purpose.** A [4]-week pilot of Saktum Check (the MCP Trust Assessment) in Partner's environment.
2. **No fee.** The pilot is free of charge. Either party may stop at any time.
3. **Partner's data.** Saktum Check runs on Partner's systems; Saktum does not receive Partner's data. Anything Partner
   chooses to share (for example an assessment report) is confidential under the NDA.
4. **Feedback.** Partner gives feedback in a weekly 30-minute call. Saktum may use feedback to improve the product.
5. **Reference.** With Partner's written approval of the wording, Saktum may name Partner as a design partner and
   use an approved quote.
6. **Software.** Saktum Check is open source (Apache-2.0); no warranty beyond that licence.
7. **Success criteria.** [inventory complete; policy enforced in one repository; one agent workflow governed; assessment delivered].
8. **Term.** From [date] to [date].
