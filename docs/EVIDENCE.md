# Evidence for the frameworks you are held to

Generated by `saktum evidence --framework all --markdown`. Saktum **supports evidence** for these obligations; it does not establish compliance, which depends on your own assessment and advisers.

## EU AI Act

Most obligations apply to high-risk AI systems; whether a system is high-risk is the organisation's assessment.

| Ref | Obligation | Coverage | Saktum evidence | Commands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Art. 9 | Risk management | ◐ partly | Risk tiers R0–R4 for servers and agents, policy ceilings, the executive assessment | `saktum check`, `saktum assess` |
| Art. 12 | Record-keeping (automatic logging) | ✓ covered | Signed, hash-chained receipts of every agent tool call, with the decision and its reason | `saktum proxy`, `saktum receipts verify` |
| Art. 13 | Transparency and information to deployers | ◐ partly | Passports, manifests and plain-language mandates | `saktum passport`, `saktum manifest create` |
| Art. 14 | Human oversight | ✓ covered | Autonomy levels, signed approvals of exact actions, revocation (the stop button) | `saktum agent approve`, `saktum agent revoke` |
| Art. 15 | Accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity | ◐ partly | Conformance and security checks of tools; containment of manipulated agents by mandates (not model accuracy) | `saktum check`, `saktum demo` |
| Art. 26 | Obligations of deployers (monitoring, logs) | ✓ covered | Monitoring of approved tools, receipts retained by the deployer, OpenTelemetry export | `saktum watch`, `saktum receipts export --otel` |
| Art. 50 | Transparency for certain AI systems | – not covered | Not in scope: disclosure to people interacting with AI is the deployer's interface |  |
